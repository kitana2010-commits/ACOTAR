The Night Court

Winter Solstice

the longest night of the year ✦ ✦ ✦

Snow settles over Velaris, and the whole city glows. Lights and garlands hang along the streets, windows burn gold against the dark, and the smell of spiced food drifts from every doorway. It is the longest night of the year, and no one spends it alone.

When & Where

On the longest night of the year, across the Night Court. Velaris is at its most festive, from the snowy streets of the city to the shops along the Palace of Thread and Jewels.

✦

How Velaris Celebrates

In the days before Solstice, the city fills with shoppers hunting for the perfect gifts, and the streets are strung with lights and evergreen.

On the night itself, friends and families gather in their homes to feast, drink and exchange presents, celebrating late into the longest night of the year.

✦

The Inner Circle

The High Lord and his inner circle spend Solstice together at the townhouse in Velaris, with a long dinner, plenty of wine and a pile of gifts to open by the fire.

The Snowball Fight

Before the festivities, Rhysand, Cassian and Azriel slip away to a cabin in the snowy mountains for a snowball fight. It is a brotherly battle, and no one fights fair.

✦

Visitor Etiquette

If you are invited into a home for Solstice, bring a gift.
Solstice is spent with those you love; the city grows quiet once the feasting begins.
