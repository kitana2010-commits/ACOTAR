The Spring Court

Calanmai

— Fire Night — ✦ ✦ ✦

Bonfires burn on the hills, and the drums carry through the woods long into the night. The air is thick with smoke and wild magic, and it is hard to tell where the music ends and your own heartbeat begins.

When & Where

Once a year, in spring, across the Spring Court. The bonfires are lit on the hillsides at nightfall, and the revelry spills into the surrounding woods.

Customs

  • Faeries gather around the bonfires to drink, dance and revel until dawn.
  • The night belongs to the drums, and their rhythm draws revelers toward the hills.
  • At the height of the night, the High Lord performs the Great Rite: a sacred union in a cave on the hill. His magic draws a chosen partner to him, and their joining pours power back into the land for the year ahead.

✦ Fair Warning ✦

Fire Night is a night of sexual freedom. The magic in the air stirs desire in everyone it touches, and revelers pair off freely by the fires and in the woods. If that is not what you came for, stay indoors.

Visitor Etiquette

  • Mortals are advised to stay indoors. The magic runs wild on Fire Night, and not every faerie at the fires is friendly.
  • The Great Rite belongs to the High Lord. Do not follow anyone to the cave.
  • Keep to the firelight. Do not wander the woods alone.
Local Tip

Stay away from the cave mouth. The closer you are to the drums, the heavier the music will compel you.
