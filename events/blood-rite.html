Travel Advisory The Illyrian Mountains

The Blood
Rite

a rite of passage, paid for in blood

The Illyrian Mountains go quiet. The war-camps keep their fires burning through the night and watch the peaks, while somewhere in the snow below Ramiel, young warriors fight their way toward the summit.

Warning

For Illyrians Only · Deadly

The Blood Rite is not a spectacle, and it is not open to outsiders. Only Illyrians may take part, and it is deadly: every year, some of those who enter never come down from the mountains.

When & Where

Once a year, in the Illyrian Mountains of the Night Court. Those taking part are left in the wilderness around Ramiel, the sacred mountain, and must reach its summit.

What It Is

The Blood Rite is the Illyrians' brutal rite of passage. Warriors in training are dropped into the mountains with no supplies, their magic bound, and given one week to reach the stone atop Ramiel.

Nothing is forbidden. Old feuds are settled, alliances are made and broken, and not everyone survives. Those who touch the stone at the summit return as full Illyrian warriors.

Visitor Etiquette

Stay off the mountains around Ramiel until the Rite is over.
The families waiting in the camps below are not there to celebrate. Give them room.
